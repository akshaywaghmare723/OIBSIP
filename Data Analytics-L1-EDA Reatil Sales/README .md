# Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to understand sales trends, customer demographics, product category performance, and customer purchasing behavior.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and identify useful business insights that can help improve sales and customer-focused strategies.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

- Dataset inspection and data cleaning
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Gender-wise and age-group analysis
- Product category sales analysis
- Quantity analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Customer spending and behavior analysis
- Additional visualizations

## Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest total sales.
- Clothing had the highest quantity sold.
- Customers aged 46-55 generated the highest sales.
- May 2023 recorded the highest monthly sales.
- Female customers generated slightly higher total sales than male customers.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus more on Electronics products.
- Promote Clothing products to increase sales volume.
- Target the 46-55 age group with suitable offers.
- Study successful sales periods such as May 2023.
- Introduce customer retention and repeat-purchase campaigns.

## Dataset Limitation

The dataset does not contain individual product names. Therefore, a Top 10 best-selling product analysis could not be performed. Product performance was analyzed using product categories instead.

## Project Structure

- Retail_Sales_Analysis.ipynb - Jupyter Notebook containing the complete analysis.
- retail_sales_dataset.csv - Retail sales dataset.
